# OreoDeck website brand handoff

Apply the approved Companion Deck identity to the website from these checked-in
files:

- `assets/brand/generated/oreodeck-mark-transparent-1024.png` — square mark
  for navigation, README-style contexts, favicon derivation, and light or dark
  backgrounds.
- `assets/brand/oreodeck-companion-lockup.svg` — horizontal lockup for the
  website footer or partner-facing contexts on a dark surface.
- `assets/brand/generated/oreodeck-social-1200x630.png` — Open Graph and social
  preview image.

Use charcoal `#181614` for dark fields, terracotta `#D46C4E` as the primary
brand color, coral `#F2A07C` for the front card, cream `#FFF4EA` for the `O`,
and amber `#FFCF8B` only for the active-profile dot.

The supporting descriptor is **A companion for Claude Code**. Do not put it
inside the `OreoDeck` wordmark. OreoDeck is an independent product: do not use
Anthropic's logo or wordmark, and do not claim endorsement or official
affiliation.
